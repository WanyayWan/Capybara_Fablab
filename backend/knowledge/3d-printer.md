## Which 3D printers does this guide cover?
This guide covers the Bambu Lab P1S and X1E printers in the 3D printing area. Both use an AMS (automated material station) on top for filament.

## How do I use the 3D printer? (full procedure)
There are six steps: power on, load filament, load the plate, insert the SD card, start the print, then remove your part and return the plate. Say "next" and I will guide you one step at a time.

## Step 1: How do I turn on the printer?
Press the power button at the bottom right corner on the back of the machine.

## Step 2: How do I load filament?
Open the AMS cover on top of the printer. Take the filament out to free the filament end. Gently push the grey tab in front of the feeder, insert the filament into the grey opening about 2 cm, then release the grey tab once the spool starts to roll. The AMS will detect the filament and start loading automatically.

## Step 3: How do I put the build plate on?
Use a clean plate. Align it with the two corners at the back, with the PLA/ABS/PETG text facing the front, and place it on the magnetic platform. Be careful: the magnet is strong and can pinch your fingers.

## Step 4: Where do I insert the SD card?
On the P1S, the SD slot is at the top left corner. On the X1E, it is on the side of the panel. Use a micro SD card of 32 GB or smaller.

## Step 5: How do I start my print?
On the printer screen, tap the folder icon, select your job file, then proceed to print.

## Step 6: How do I remove my print?
Take the build plate off and flex it gently to pop your model off. Make sure the plate is clean before putting it back on the printer.

## How do I eject the SD card?
Press the card down. The slot is spring-loaded and will push the card out.

## What do I need to do when I finish?
Return the clean build plate to the printer and return all tools provided by the Fab Lab.

## What filament types are supported?
The build plate is marked for PLA, ABS and PETG.

## What is the maximum SD card size?
32 GB micro SD.

## Where is the lost and found?
There is a Lost & Found box on the shelf in the 3D printing area.

## When should I call staff? (demo content)
Call staff if the AMS shows an error, the filament will not load, the printer jams or makes grinding noises, the plate is damaged, or you are unsure about any step. Do not open or repair the printer yourself.
