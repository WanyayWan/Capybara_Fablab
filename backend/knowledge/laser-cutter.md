## How do I use the laser cutter? (full procedure)
There are six steps: check you are authorised, turn on the extraction, load your material, set up your file, run a test, then run your job and stay with the machine. Say "next" and I will guide you one step at a time.

## Step 1: Am I allowed to use the laser cutter?
You must complete the laser cutter training and be authorised by Fab Lab staff first. I can't confirm your authorisation, so check with staff if you are unsure.

## Step 2: What do I turn on first?
Turn on the fume extraction and air assist before you start cutting. Never cut with the extraction off.

## Step 3: How do I load my material?
Place your material flat on the bed, away from the edges. Make sure it is not warped and is weighed down or held flat if needed. Then set the focus for your material's thickness as shown in your training.

## Step 4: How do I set up my file?
Use thin lines for cutting and filled shapes for engraving. Check the colour and line settings your machine uses with staff, then start from the material preset for your material.

## Step 5: Why should I do a test cut?
Settings vary between machines and material batches. Cut a small square in a corner first to check it cuts through cleanly before running the full job.

## Step 6: Can I leave the laser cutter while it runs?
No. Never leave the laser cutter unattended. Stay and watch the whole job, because materials can catch fire.

## What materials can I cut?
Common safe materials are cast acrylic, plywood, cardboard, paper, and natural fabrics. Check with staff before using anything else.

## What materials are banned?
Never cut PVC or vinyl, because they release toxic chlorine gas. Also avoid polycarbonate, ABS, HDPE, foam, fibreglass, and coated carbon fibre. If you don't know what a material is, don't cut it.

## Can I cut PVC or vinyl on the laser cutter?
No. Never cut PVC or vinyl. They release toxic chlorine gas that harms people and damages the machine. If you're not sure what a material is, don't cut it and ask staff.

## Can I cut metal (aluminium, steel, copper, brass) on the laser cutter?
No. The CO2 laser cutters can't cut metal, including aluminium, steel, copper and brass. The metal shop has a fibre laser and a waterjet for metal parts. Ask staff about those.

## Is a small flame normal?
Small flashes where the laser touches the material can be normal. A flame that keeps burning is not. Stop the job and call staff right away.

## What do I do if there is a fire?
Stop the job, keep the lid closed, and alert staff immediately. Only use a fire extinguisher if you have been trained.

## When can I open the lid after cutting?
Wait a few seconds after the job ends for the fumes to clear before opening the lid.

## What do I do when I finish?
Remove your parts and scraps, clean the bed, turn off the machine as trained, and return any tools.

## When should I call staff?
Call staff if the machine shows an error, the cut doesn't go through, you see sustained flames, you're unsure about a material, or you haven't been trained. Do not adjust or repair the machine yourself.
