## Where is the Fab Lab?
Building 5, Levels 1 and 2, SUTD, 8 Somapah Road.

## How do I contact the Fab Lab?
Email fablab@sutd.edu.sg, or drop by Building 5 and speak to the staff.

## Who can use the Fab Lab?
Every SUTD student can use the lab, but you must be trained and authorised first.

## How do I get access to a machine?
Three steps. First, complete the safety and equipment training for the machine. Second, get authorisation from Fab Lab staff. Third, book a time slot in the Fab Lab booking system on the SUTD intranet.

## How do I book a machine?
Use the Fab Lab booking system on the SUTD intranet. If you are off campus, connect to the SUTD VPN first.

## Am I allowed to use this machine?
I can't confirm that. Only Fab Lab staff can authorise you. If you have not completed the training for this machine, please speak to staff first.

## What shops does the Fab Lab have?
Metal shop, wood shop, 3D printing lab, electronics lab, laser lab, welding shop, sewing section, two makerspaces, and a large assembly space.

## What can the laser cutters cut?
The laser lab has CO2 laser cutters and engravers for materials like acrylic, plywood, cardboard and fabric. Check with staff before using any other material.

## What 3D printers are available?
The 3D printing lab has FDM, SLA, SLS and PolyJet printers.

## Can I weld on my own?
No. Welding requires training and supervision.

## Where is the full list of machines?
On SUTD Central, under Undergraduate, then Fabrication Lab. It is only available on the intranet.
